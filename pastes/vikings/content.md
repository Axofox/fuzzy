# Viking Vengeance — Alliance Guide

Every two weeks, Tuesday and Thursday. 20 waves of AI Vikings attack alliance members' cities. We defend.

---

## The one thing that decides your score

**Points come from Vikings dying in your troops' presence — anywhere.** Not just your own city.

The points for defending your own city are fixed. Once you have enough troops at home to survive, everything else sitting there earns you nothing. Send those troops to reinforce other members and you collect kill points from every city they fight at.

**Troops at home beyond what you need are wasted troops.**

---

## Troop setup

**90% Infantry / 10% Cavalry / Archers last.**

This is the opposite of Bear Trap. Infantry does the work here, cavalry supports, archers are the lowest priority.

To defend a wave you must kill at least 50% of the attacking troops. Kill 100% and you get 100% of the available points — so overkill isn't wasted, it's the goal.

---

## What to do

**1. Keep the minimum at home.** Enough to hold your own city, no more.

**2. Reinforce everyone you can.** Prioritise members who are **online** — waves 7, 14 and 17 only attack online members, so troops parked at an offline member's city do nothing during those waves.

**3. Waves 10 and 20 hit the alliance HQ.** These are the big ones. Get troops into the HQ before they land.

**4. Use the Members tab during the event** to see where reinforcements are needed.

---

## For leadership

**Pick difficulty on clean clears, not pride.** The right level is the highest one we can clear repeatedly without attendance panic, healing panic, or timing chaos. Agree a fallback difficulty before we start.

**Before the event opens:**
- Confirm target difficulty and fallback
- Rally leads prepare marches, heroes and ratios in advance
- Post a shared UTC start time
- Assign a small number of shot callers rather than everyone calling their own timing
- Check hospital capacity for the full window, not just the first few waves

---

## The short version

**Empty your city. Reinforce online members. Be at the HQ for waves 10 and 20. Send infantry.**
