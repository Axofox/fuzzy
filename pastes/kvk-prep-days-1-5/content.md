# TIMING — THIS WHERE YOU COULD LOSE POINTS

**Intel Missions.** Scores on Days 1, 3 and 5. Start a mission late on Day 5 of the week *before*, and complete it the moment Day 1 opens. Same trick between Day 2→3 and Day 4→5. Never complete an Intel Mission on Day 2 or Day 4 — it scores nothing those days. Let it sit.

**Troop training.** Start a long batch on Day 3 so it finishes on Day 4. Better: queue it, then hold your training speedups until Day 4 and use them to complete the batch — you score the speedup minutes *and* the trained troops on the same day.

**Construction.** Start a long build on Day 5 of the previous cycle, then finish it with speedups on Day 1.

**Research.** Same principle — have something long already running so your research speedups have somewhere to go on Days 1, 2 and 5.

**Gathering.** Send marches out before midnight so they're returning during a scoring day. Gathering scores on every day, so this is pure filler — but keep marches moving constantly.

**Governor Charms.** If you're close to a level breakpoint, push through it on Day 1 in one go. The milestone bonus only pays when you cross the level.

---

[red]# DON'T[/red]

- **Don't complete Intel on Days 2 and 4.** Hold last two missions.
- **Don't spend Governor Gear materials before Day 5.** No other day scores them.
- **Don't burn speedups early in the day.** If you'd hit the 200k cap anyway, save them for a day you're short.
- **Beast hunting scores nothing.** Don't waste stamina.
