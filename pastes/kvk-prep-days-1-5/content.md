# KVK PREP — THE 5 DAYS

Target: **200,000 personal points per day.** That's three daily chests plus a State Medal.

---

## BEFORE DAY 1 OPENS

Do all of this on the last day of matchmaking:

- Start a **long building upgrade** and leave it running. Your Day 1 speedups need somewhere to go.
- Start a **long research** for the same reason.
- Queue an **Intel Mission** so you can finish it the moment Day 1 opens.
- Activate the **Wolf pet buff** and use your **Double Time Decree**.
- **Empty your hospital.**
- Send gathering marches out.

Using items before your buffs are active is the most common way people waste an entire stockpile.

---

## Day 1 — City Construction
- Truegold on a building — 2,000
- Truegold Dust on tech research — 1,000
- Intel Mission — 6,000
- Governor Charm max score +1 — 70, plus a one-time bonus for crossing a charm level
- Speedups (construction / research / training) — 30 per minute
- Gathering — 2 per 1,000 bread/wood, per 200 stone, per 50 iron

**Points come from *spending* the Truegold and the speedups, not from the building finishing.** That's why you need something already running.

**→ HOLD FOR TOMORROW:** don't spend Hero Roulette spins or Hero Shards today. They score on Day 2.

---

## Day 2 — Basic Skills Up
- Hero Roulette spin — 8,000
- Mythic Hero Shard — 3,040 · Epic — 1,220 · Rare — 350
- Truegold Dust — 1,000
- Master Emblems and Manuscripts
- Research speedups, gathering

**→ HOLD FOR TOMORROW:** if you have an Intel Mission ready, **do not finish it today.** Intel scores nothing on Day 2. Let it sit and claim it tomorrow for 6,000. Also hold your Taming Marks.

---

## Day 3 — Pet Training
- Advanced Taming Mark — 15,000
- Common Taming Mark — 1,150
- Pet Advancement +1 — 50
- Intel Mission — 6,000
- Hero Roulette and shards score again today
- Speedups, gathering

**→ HOLD FOR TOMORROW:** start a **long troop training batch** today so it's running when Day 4 opens. Do **not** use your training speedups yet. Hold Mithril, Forgehammers and Widgets.

---

## Day 4 — Hero Development
- Mithril — 40,000
- Widgets — 8,000 · Forgehammers — 4,000
- **Train troops:** T9 = 45 each, T8 = 35, T7 = 25, T6 = 18, T5 = 12
- Training speedups — best day for them
- Gathering

Dump your training speedups into yesterday's batch. You score the speedup minutes *and* the trained troops on the same day.

**→ HOLD FOR TOMORROW:** an Intel Mission — it scores nothing today. And keep back some Mithril, Widgets, Forgehammers and Taming Marks; they all score again tomorrow.

---

## Day 5 — Power Boost
The widest day. Everything left over scores here.

- Mithril — 40,000
- Advanced Taming Mark — 15,000
- Widgets — 8,000 · Intel — 6,000 · Forgehammers — 4,000 · Truegold — 2,000
- **Governor Gear — 36 per point. TODAY ONLY. No other day scores it.**
- Pet Advancement, speedups, gathering

**Before the day ends:** start a long building upgrade for the next cycle.

---

## THE THREE RULES

1. **Never finish an Intel Mission on Day 2 or Day 4.** It pays zero those days. Hold it.
2. **Never spend Governor Gear materials before Day 5.** No other day scores them.
3. **Keep a build, a research and a training batch always running.** Saved speedups are worthless with nothing to spend them on.

Beast hunting scores nothing all week. Don't waste stamina on it.
