**Also save — lower per-unit, but volume adds up**
- Construction, research, training and Master Skill speedups — 30 points per minute. Every day scores these, but training speedups are best on Day 4.
- Governor Charm materials — 70 points per max-score point, plus a one-time bonus for crossing a charm level. Day 1 is best. If you're close to a level breakpoint, push through it on one day rather than spreading materials.
- Governor Gear materials — 36 points per max-score point. **Day 5 only.** No other day scores this.
- Pet Advancement — 50 points per point. Day 3 or 5.
- Troops to train — Day 4 is the day. T11 = 75 pts each, T10 = 60, T9 = 45, T8 = 35, T7 = 25, T6 = 18, T5 = 12. Bank the resources and speedups, then train in bulk on Day 4.
- Gathering — 2 points per 1,000 bread or wood, per 200 stone, per 50 iron. Keep marches out all week; best on Days 2 and 4.

**Do before Day 1 starts, not after**
- Activate Wolf pet buff
- Use Double Time Decree
- Apply for Chief Minister if the kingdom coordinates it
- Queue Watchtower intel so it rolls over into Day 1
- Empty your hospital

Using items *before* the buffs are active is the single most common way people waste their whole stockpile.

**Two things worth knowing**

Day 5 is the widest scoring day — Mithril, Tempered Truegold, Taming Marks, Widgets, Forgehammers, Truegold, Intel, Pet Advancement and Governor Gear all score. If someone is unsure when to spend something, Day 5 is rarely wrong.
