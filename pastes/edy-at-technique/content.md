# Edy's AT Battle Technique

The principle is to learn how to defend the leader in battle the best way possible. Not because your casualties don't matter, but because the damage is far greater if he falls.

You will not have time to arrange heroes once a rally timer is running. Do this as soon as possible. Your presets can be used for Bear Trap too.

| Preset | Heroes | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| **Attack 1** | Chenko + 2 | Joining the attack rally |
| **Attack 2** | Amane + 2 | Joining the attack rally |
| **Defense 1** | Howard + 2 | Garrison (building) |
| **Defense 2** | Gordon + 2 | Reinforce (leader) |

## During the rally — 3 marches, in this order

**1. Join the rally**
Lead with **Chenko**. If Chenko is unavailable, use Amane.

**2. Send Garrison — with Howard**
Do this the moment the troops leave the leader's base.

**3. Reinforce the Leader — with Gordon**
Immediately after step 2.

Order matters. The rally has a countdown. Garrison and reinforce do not.

## The swap

Howard takes Chenko's place at the target.

Time it so Howard arrives as close to the target as possible, then **recall Chenko about 3 seconds before Howard lands.**

That's it! 

Bellow you'll find further explanation that could help understand the "why".

The point is that your attack heroes are never sitting in the building when the enemy can hit them. Chenko has no defensive value — anything that lands on her is damage you took for nothing. Howard is built to absorb that hit. So Chenko leaves, Howard arrives, and the position is never uncovered.

Get the timing wrong and you lose it. Recall too early and there's a gap the enemy can walk into. Recall too late and Chenko eats a hit she was never meant to take. **Watch both march timers, not just one.**

## After a hit

If you take casualties, you can always send your defense heroes again just to replace the wounded. The hero comes back to base with fewer troops — that's normal, and it's not a reason to stop sending. A thinner march holding the position beats an empty one.

## Why these heroes

Howard and Gordon go in the defensive marches because that is what they are for. Howard's expedition skill is purely defensive: poor value in a rally, good value in a garrison.

Chenko and Amane are best for joining because their first expedition skills are flat offensive buffs that fire every time — no chance roll, no condition. Only your first hero's first expedition skill counts when you join, so leading with either of them puts a guaranteed damage increase into the rally. And their buffs come from different stacking groups, which means a rally with both in it hits harder than a rally with two of the same.

Possible troops ratios:
| March | Ratio | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Attack join** (Chenko or Amane) | **50 / 20 / 30** | The standard rally split. Enough front line to absorb the first waves, archers as the damage engine, cavalry to reach their back line. |
| **Garrison** (Howard) | **60 / 20 / 20** | Archers lose their edge defending — low defensive stats. Shift the weight to infantry. |
| **Reinforce leader** (Gordon) | **60 / 20 / 20** | Same reasoning. You're keeping the leader standing, not maximizing damage. |
